## What is noise and what is not

All of it is noise statistically: 85 shows, 23 clicks, one day, mostly one person. Do not pick a winner from this.
Two things are worth believing anyway:
- The mug gets the most clicks (14 of 25 shows), but it is also the card shown first and the one Kevin tests most.
  Not evidence yet.
- "ad" beats "sponsored" 15/43 against 4/41. The gap is big enough (about 35% against 10%) to be unlikely
  chance even at this size, but it comes from the same test rig. Do not switch labels on this alone. Keep the
  test running; it costs nothing.
- Everything else (variant, wording, GPU against CPU votes) has 10 or fewer events per row. Ignore.

4. **Add Printful or Printify only once Kevin has their links.** Both still have empty `url`, so they never show.

## Problems that cost users

- **iPhone crashes.** 3 of the 6 crashes are iPhones: 2 while loading the model, 1 while writing the PNG for a
  16 MP photo. That is 3 crashes against about 34 iOS views. Known memory limits (CLAUDE.md); the 16 MP PNG is the
  one with a concrete fix path (smaller canvas for the export on iOS).
- **GPU quality.** 4 of 5 GPU votes are 👎 (3 first results, 1 retry). The engine is `gpu` (ISNet), not BiRefNet,
  so these are the phone-and-fallback path, or a desktop that picked ISNet. Check why a desktop would run ISNet:
  `gpu-birefnet` has no rating at all yet. Too few votes to act on; watch it.
- **Desktop "model" crash (1) and GPU-BiRefNet "other" (1).** One each. Not a pattern yet.
- Nothing recorded for ads breaking: no `ad-failed` or `mug-failed` crash steps.
