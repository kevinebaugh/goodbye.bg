goodbye.bg👋

Remove the background from 100 photos at once

Free, no credits, no account. The photos are cut out on your device.

  1. Open goodbye.bg on a computer. The first visit downloads the model once.
  2. Drop a folder, a ZIP, or a selection of photos on the page. Up to 100 at a time on computers, 50 on phones. They appear at once and are worked through from the top left.
  3. Click Download all. You get one zip, each file named after its photo.

Remove backgrounds in bulk

How fast it is

On a computer with a recent graphics chip, each photo takes a fraction of a second, so 100 photos are done in a minute or two. On older computers and on phones each photo takes a few seconds. Nothing is uploaded, so your connection speed does not matter once the page has loaded.

The same settings for every photo

Set one photo the way you want it, then use Apply to all:

  • A background: transparent, white, black, any colour, the photo’s own background blurred, or a picture of your own
  • A size: the original, square, 4:5, 16:9, or your own up to 5000 pixels
  • A margin around the subject, and a soft shadow under it
  • WebP instead of PNG or JPG, where your browser can make it

Photos with a transparent background come back as PNG, photos on a background as JPG.

What goes in

JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, TIFF, GIF, SVG and camera RAW files (the preview the camera stored inside). Folders and ZIPs are opened for you; files that are not pictures are skipped.

More than 100

Add the next batch when the first is done and downloaded. Between visits the page keeps the newest 50 photos on this device; older ones make way for new ones.

What it costs

Nothing. There are no credits and no size limits beyond your device’s own: results come back at the photo’s own resolution, up to 16 megapixels in Safari and on iPhone and 64 elsewhere.
