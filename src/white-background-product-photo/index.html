A white background for product photos

Pure white, square, an even margin, a soft shadow. Free, and the photos stay on your device.

  1. Open goodbye.bg and add your product photos. Up to 100 at a time on computers, 50 on phones.
  2. Pick white. The round button next to Download opens the backgrounds. White is pure white (255, 255, 255).
  3. Set the size and margin. The sliders button sets square, 4:5, 16:9 or your own size up to 5000 pixels, the margin around the product, and a soft shadow under it.
  4. Apply to all, then Download all. Every photo gets the same look. You get one zip of JPGs.

Make a white background

Why white and square

Most marketplaces show listings in a square grid, and several ask for a plain white background on the main picture. Amazon asks for pure white. Check your marketplace’s current rules for the exact size and how much of the picture the product should fill; the margin setting controls that.

The margin

The margin is measured from the product, not from the old photo, so every listing lines up even when the originals were framed differently. Choose none, 4%, 10% or 20% of the product’s longer side.

The shadow

A cut-out on pure white can look as if it floats. The shadow setting adds a soft one under the product. Leave it off where the rules ask for nothing but the product.

File type and size

Photos on a background come back as JPG, which marketplaces accept and which keeps files small. WebP is there too, where your browser can make it. Results are at the photo’s own resolution unless you set a size.

What works well, and what does not

  • Works well: products with a clear outline on a plain or contrasting surface.
  • Harder: glass and other see-through things, white products on a white surface, and very thin parts such as wires and chains.
  • Reflections stay. A mirror reflection under the product is usually kept as part of it. Shoot on a matte surface if you can.

If a piece is wrong, the pencil under the photo opens an editor: tap a piece to drop or keep it, or use the erase and restore brush.

Other backgrounds

The same button gives a transparent PNG, black, any colour, the photo’s own background blurred, or a picture of your own, such as a tabletop or a room.
