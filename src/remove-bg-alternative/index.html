Questions

Is goodbye.bg free?

Yes. Every photo, at high resolution, with no account, no credits, and no watermark.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The background removal runs in your browser, and photos from your device stay there. Clicking one of the product ads takes that cut-out to Zazzle, so it’s on the product when you arrive: our server holds it until Zazzle has it, an hour at most. If you paste a link to a public image, our server fetches that image for you and keeps nothing.

What do I get back?

A PNG at the photo's own resolution (up to 16 megapixels in Safari and on iPhone, 64 elsewhere) with a transparent background, cropped to the subject unless you switch the crop off. An animated GIF comes back as an animated GIF. Download one photo or all of them as a zip.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in any modern browser. A phone takes a few seconds per photo. On iPhone, press and hold a result to save it to Photos.

Is there an API like remove.bg's?

No. goodbye.bg is a web page for people. The source code is open, so you can run it yourself.
