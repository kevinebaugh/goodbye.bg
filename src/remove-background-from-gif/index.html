Remove the background from an animated GIF

Every frame is cut out on your device, and it comes back as an animated GIF with a transparent background.

  1. Open goodbye.bg. The first visit downloads the model once.
  2. Add the GIF. Choose it, drop it on the page, or paste a link to it.
  3. Download. The card plays the result. Download saves it as a GIF; on iPhone, press and hold it to save.

Remove a GIF’s background

How long it takes

Each frame is a photo of its own, so a GIF takes as long as that many photos. A 10-frame GIF takes about 6 seconds on a computer with a recent graphics chip.

Long GIFs

Up to 100 frames are cut out on a computer with a recent graphics chip, 16 on phones and older computers. A longer GIF keeps every second, third or n-th frame and plays for the same length of time.

What to expect

  • Hard edges. A GIF pixel is either see-through or not, so soft edges such as hair become hard. That is a limit of the GIF format, not of goodbye.bg.
  • The whole frame. GIFs are not cropped to the subject, so every frame keeps its place.
  • A steady subject works best. Each frame is judged on its own. When the subject is clear in every frame the result is smooth; when it is small or blurred in some frames, it can flicker.
  • Transparent only. The background goes; nothing is put in its place.

Where a transparent GIF helps

It sits cleanly on any page, slide or chat colour, with no box around the subject.
