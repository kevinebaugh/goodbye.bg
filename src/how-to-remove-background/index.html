How to remove a background from an image

Three steps, free, and the photo stays on your device.

  1. Open goodbye.bg. The first visit downloads the model once. Later visits start faster.
  2. Add photos. Choose them, drop them or a whole folder or ZIP on the page, paste one, or paste a link to an image. JPG, PNG, HEIC, WebP, GIF, TIFF and camera RAW all work. Up to 100 at a time on computers, 50 on phones; up to the newest 50 are kept on this device.
  3. Download. Each photo comes back as a PNG at the photo's own resolution (up to 16 megapixels in Safari and on iPhone, 64 elsewhere) with a transparent background, cropped to the subject. An animated GIF comes back as an animated GIF. Use Download all for a zip.

Remove a background

Transparent PNG

A transparent PNG works for logos, stickers, slides, and anything you place on another background.

What works well

  • People, kids, and pets
  • Products
  • Objects with a clear outline, like bikes and furniture

What is harder

  • Close-ups where the background is soft and blurred
  • Subjects that blend into a busy background of the same color
  • Busy scenes, like a car interior or a small pet in a dark yard
  • Fine single strands of hair

For these, a photo with more contrast between subject and background gives a cleaner cut.

On a phone

Open goodbye.bg in Safari or Chrome and tap Choose photos. On Android you can also share a photo to goodbye.bg after adding it to your home screen. Each photo takes a few seconds. On iPhone, press and hold a result to save it to Photos.
