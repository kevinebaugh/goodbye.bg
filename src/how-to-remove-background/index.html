How to remove a background from an image

Three steps, free, and the photo stays on your device.

  1. Open goodbye.bg. The first visit downloads the model once. Later visits start faster.
  2. Add photos. Choose them, drop them on the page, paste one, or paste a link to an image. Add as many as you like.
  3. Download. Each photo comes back as a PNG at the photo's own resolution (up to 16 megapixels in Safari and on iPhone, 64 elsewhere) with a transparent background, cropped to the subject. The crop icon on a result switches back to the whole frame. Use Download all for a zip.

Remove a background

Transparent PNG

A transparent PNG works for logos, stickers, slides, and anything you place on another background.

What works well

  • People in front of a crowd or a busy scene
  • Pets and products
  • Objects with a clear outline, like bikes and furniture

What is harder

  • Close-ups where the background is soft and blurred
  • Subjects that blend into a busy background of the same color
  • Fine single strands of hair

For these, a photo with more contrast between subject and background gives a cleaner cut.

On a phone

Open goodbye.bg in Safari or Chrome and tap Choose photos. On Android you can also share a photo to goodbye.bg after adding it to your home screen. Phones take longer than computers because the work happens on the phone.
